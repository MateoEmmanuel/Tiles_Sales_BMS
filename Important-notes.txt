Tiles-Selling-Business-Management-System
**********************************************************************************************
On a new PC, they simply:
Windows:
    Download/clone the project
    Double-click setup.bat
    Wait for it to complete
    Run flutter run
macOS/Linux:
    Download/clone the project
    Run: bash setup.sh
    Wait for it to complete
    Run: flutter run

    - Inventory Management System
        ~ Live Stock Status
            < Quanttity on Hand
                "The exact number of boxes or square meters/feet currently 
                sitting physically in your warehouse or storage."

            < Allocated Stock
                "Stock that is already sold to a customer but has not been 
                picked up or delivered yet."

            < Available Stock
                "The true number you can safely sell to a new customer 
                (Quantity on Hand minus Allocated Stock basically)."
            
        ~ Unit of Measure Converter System
            "A tool that automatically converts Boxes to Square Meters 
            (Sqm) / Square Feet (Sqft) and vice versa. Customers buy by 
            area, but you stock by the box."

        ~ Smart Alerts and Reordering
            < Reorder Point
                "A safety threshold you set for each tile variant."

            < Low Stock Alerts
                "Automatic visual warnings when a tile's available stock dips 
                below its reorder point, prompting you to restock."

            < Out-Of-Stock Alerts
            "Automatic visual warnings when a tile's available stock dips 
                zero, prompting you to restock ASAP."

        ~ Restock Logging
            "just the normal logging of new tiles in the warehouse or storage."

        ~ Stock Movement Logs
            < Restock Logs
            < Usage/Sales Logs (Quanttity Sold)
            < Adjustment Logs
            < Damaged/Defective Stock Logs
            < Returned Stock Logs

    - Sales & Transaction Management
        ~ Sales Transaction Recording
        ~ Sales Invoice/Receipt Generation
        ~ Customer Order/Reservation Management
            "It's simply the staff recording: ["Customer Juan bought 20 boxes, 
            but hasn't picked them up yet."] That's exactly what your Allocated
             Stock concept is for."

            < Pending
            < Reserved
            < Paid
            < Ready for Pickup
            < Delivered/Released
            < Cancelled

        ~ Payment Recording
        ~ Discount Management
        ~ Sales History

Proposed System in Groups:
> Admin
"The Admin handles system administration, staff access, 
master data, inventory oversight, and business monitoring."

    ~ User & Access Management
        < User/Staff Information Management
            + User & Access Management
            + Create/Update Staff Accounts
            + Assign User Roles
            + Manage Permissions
            + Activate/Deactivate Accounts

        < Audit Trail / Activity Log
            + User who performed the action
            + Action performed
            + Affected record
            + Date/Time
            + Previous/New value when applicable

    ~ Product Management
        < Tile Products & Specification Management
            + Tile Name/Code
            + Brand
            + Design/Color
            + Size
            + Thickness
            + Finish
            + Pieces per Box
            + Coverage per Box
            + Price per Box
            + Price per Sqm/Sqft
