TILES SELLING BUSINESS MANAGEMENT SYSTEM
FIREBASE FIRESTORE DATA DICTIONARY

This is a Firebase Firestore data dictionary. Each collection contains documents.
Firebase Authentication manages passwords and login credentials. Passwords must
not be stored in the Firestore users collection.

Firebase Authentication
-----------------------
Provider: Email and Password
Account roles: admin_owner, office_staff, cashier

The Firebase Authentication UID is used as the document ID in the users
collection. The UID is also saved in createdBy, updatedBy, and performedBy fields.

Data types used: String, Number, Boolean, Timestamp, Map, and Reference.

SCOPE DECISIONS FOR THIS PRIVATE OFFICE SYSTEM
-----------------------------------------------
This application is an internal office tool, not a public customer portal.
Collections should be added only when the related office workflow is actually
needed.

- suppliers: Keep. Office staff need supplier records for sourcing and restocking.
- customers: Optional. Keep only if the office records customer names for sales,
    reservations, invoices, delivery, or sales history. It is not for public login.
- pending_reservation_orders: Optional. Add only if the office tracks customer
    reservations or pending customer requests. The name clearly identifies this
    as a pending or reserved customer order collection.
- purchase_orders: Removed. Supplier ordering remains manual by phone, in person,
    or email. The app may record the resulting stock receipt later.
- payments: Optional. Add when the system records customer payments or balances.
- notifications: In-app operational notifications only. They show low-stock,
    out-of-stock, or approval warnings for office, cashier, or admin_owner role
    categories. They are not personal messages and do not send SMS.
- app_settings: Optional. Owner/Admin can edit all settings. Office staff can edit
    selected operational settings such as display and low-stock preferences.
    Firebase security rules should enforce these permissions later.

Recommended first office version:
users, products, inventory, suppliers, stock_movements, and audit_logs.

The office can continue ordering from suppliers manually by phone, in person,
or email. Automated email ordering is not required and should only be added if
the business later confirms that suppliers accept structured email orders.

Payment note:
If amountPaid is less than totalAmount, the transaction remains recorded and
balanceDue stores the unpaid amount for the customer. A later payment updates
both the payment record and the customer's balanceDue.

ROLE ACCESS SUMMARY
===============================================================================

Admin/Owner:
- Full access to all business collections
- Create and disable user accounts
- Assign roles and manage permissions
- Approve inventory and damage records
- View reports and audit logs

Office Staff:
- Manage products, customers, suppliers, and reservations
- View and update inventory where permitted
- View business records and reports
- Cannot manage user roles or delete audit logs

Cashier:
- View products and prices
- Create sales and record payments
- View customer information needed for sales
- Process customer reservations
- Cannot manage users, suppliers, settings, or audit logs

Recommended implementation order:
1. Firebase Authentication and users
2. products and inventory
3. customers and suppliers
4. sales and payments
5. stock_movements
6. pending_reservation_orders, if reservations are needed
7. audit_logs, notifications, and app_settings
